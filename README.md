**Inside Out** is a 2015 animated movie produced by Pixar Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. It is an *animation, adventure, comedy, and family* movie that explores the importance of emotions and how they affect our everyday lives. The story follows a young girl named Riley as she experiences major changes in her life while five emotions inside her mind try to guide her through these challenges.

### Movie Information

- **Title:** Inside Out
- **Release Year:** 2015
- **Genre:** Animation, Adventure, Comedy, Family
- **Director:** Pete Docter
- **Production Studio:** Pixar Animation Studios

---
The movie **Inside Out** tells the story of an 11-year-old girl named Riley Andersen who moves from Minnesota to San Francisco with her family. Inside her mind, five emotions named Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, and Disgust control how she reacts to different situations. Joy tries her best to keep Riley happy and protect her important memories. However, things become complicated when Joy and Sadness accidentally get separated from the headquarters of Riley's mind.

Without Joy and Sadness, Riley begins to struggle with her emotions and finds it difficult to adjust to her new environment. Meanwhile, Joy and Sadness travel through different parts of Riley's mind to find their way back. Along their journey, they discover the importance of memories, friendship, and accepting different emotions. Joy eventually realizes that sadness is also necessary because it helps people express their feelings and receive comfort from others.

The movie teaches us that *every emotion has a purpose* and that growing up involves experiencing both happiness and sadness.

---

### Main Characters

1. **Joy**
   - Joy is the happy and energetic emotion inside Riley's mind. She always tries to make Riley feel positive and believes that happiness is the most important emotion.

2. **Sadness**
   - Sadness is a gentle and emotional character who often feels left out. As the story progresses, she shows that sadness is important for understanding feelings and connecting with others.

3. **Anger**
   - Anger is the short-tempered emotion who reacts strongly when things do not go as planned. He represents Riley's frustration and sense of fairness.

4. **Fear**
   - Fear is a nervous and cautious character who constantly worries about possible dangers. His main responsibility is to keep Riley safe from harmful situations.

5. **Disgust**
   - Disgust is a confident and outspoken emotion who protects Riley from unpleasant experiences and embarrassing situations.

6. **Riley Andersen**
   - Riley is the main human character of the movie. She is an 11-year-old girl who struggles to adjust to her new life after moving to another city with her family.

### Why I Chose This Movie

I chose **Inside Out** because it is one of my favorite animated movies. I like how it combines entertainment with meaningful lessons about emotions, family, and growing up. The movie reminds me that it is normal to experience different feelings and that we should not always force ourselves to be happy.

### Three Things I Liked About the Movie

1. **The Story and Message**
   - I liked how the movie explains complicated emotions in a simple and creative way. It shows that happiness and sadness are both important parts of life.

2. **The Characters**
   - Each emotion has a unique personality that makes the movie interesting and entertaining. My favorite character is Joy because of her positive attitude, but I also appreciate Sadness because of her important role in the story.

3. **The Animation and Creativity**
   - The colorful animation and creative representation of Riley's mind are amazing. I especially liked how memories, dreams, and personality islands were shown throughout the movie.
